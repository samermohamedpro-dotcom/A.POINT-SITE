    The way you already do. No card gateway, no commission, no platform between you and her.

Asked before you ask it

Questions people ask

€79 a month, setup €890, or €790 a year — do I pay both?

No — never both. There are two payments, and only two. The setup fee, paid once when we start. Then the subscription, which is either €79 every month OR €790 once a year — and €790 is ten months' worth, so paying yearly gives you two months free. You choose one or the other. The pricing page shows the first-year total both ways.

How long until I'm live?

It depends on two things: your level — Foundation is the quickest, Signature the longest, because more is built around you — and how complete your materials are when they reach me. So I do not quote a generic delay. The day I have your seven items, I give you your go-live date in writing, and it is never more than 30 days. If I miss it, the setup fee comes back.

What if I already have a website?

Keep it. We start at Operations and build the back office behind what you already have — the app, the stock, the sales. If one day you want the ordering site too, it is there.

Do I need to be in Europe or the Gulf?

No. If your customers use WhatsApp, it works. There is no payment integration and no marketplace approval, so there is nothing country-specific to block you. Your setup is in your currency and your time zone.

Who owns the site and the data?

Your data is yours: products, recipes, customers, orders, sales history, photos, texts. You can export all of it at any time, and I hand it over completely if you leave. The system itself — the site engine and the app — stays mine; you use it as long as you subscribe, like any software service. That is what makes it possible to keep running and improving it for everyone on it.
