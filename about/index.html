One person, one system

I built this for my own family first

A.Point Advisory is not an agency and not a template shop. It is one system, proven in a real kitchen, offered to other bakers by the person who built it.

  1. 01

    It started with a real problem

    My wife bakes at home in Doha — madeleines, cookies, cakes, made to order, in small batches, six days a week. The orders came in through WhatsApp. The stock lived in her head. And neither of us could have told you, honestly, what a month actually earned.

  2. 02

    So I built the thing that was missing

    A site that takes orders and sends them straight into WhatsApp, where her customers already were. Then a private app for the stock, the schedule and the real numbers. Then the accounting that turns "money came in" into "this is what is left".

  3. 03

    Then reality broke it, repeatedly

    A price changed and rewrote last month. A photo covered the text on one phone and not another. A box promised a cake it no longer contained. Every one of those became a rule, and an automated check that makes sure it never happens again. There are more than nineteen hundred of them now.

  4. 04

    That is what is for sale

    Not an idea, not a demo. The system that survived all of it, installed and run for you. The mistakes are already caught — you get the version that comes after them.

The rules I set for myself

What I will never do

Sell you hours

There is no hourly rate here. You buy a system and a result, at a price you can read before we speak.

Build one-offs

The levels are the product. If what you need would help every bakery on the system, it goes into the product and everyone gets it — several things in Complete started that way.

Discount to win you

The prices are the prices, for everyone, and they do not go up on you later. What is free is the call, the recommendation, and the guarantee.

Invent a testimonial

There is one client so far, and it is my own family. When there are more, you will hear their words, not mine.

Hold your data hostage

Export it whenever you like. If you leave, I hand over everything.

Use your customers as a demo

Never. The screens on this site show sample data, with names cut to initials and numbers hidden.
