    Questions about money

    €79 a month, setup €890, or €790 a year — do I pay both?

    No — never both. There are two payments, and only two. The setup fee, paid once when we start. Then the subscription, which is either €79 every month OR €790 once a year — and €790 is ten months' worth, so paying yearly gives you two months free. You choose one or the other. The pricing page shows the first-year total both ways.

    Why is there a setup fee at all?

    Because the first month is where the real work is: your products, prices, photos, delivery zones and hours go into the system, your site and back office are built around them, and everything is tested before you go live. The subscription pays for running it afterwards — hosting, updates, backups, support — not for building it. Charging setup separately is what keeps the monthly price low.

    What does “you only pay the difference in setup” mean?

    Say you start at Foundation, setup €390, and six months later you want Operations, setup €890. You pay €500 — the difference — and your subscription goes from €39 to €79. You never pay setup twice for the same work.

    Is my advertising budget included?

    No. Signature covers ad strategy and campaign structure — the setting-up — but the money that goes to Instagram or Google is yours and is paid by you. The Signature sticker set is illustrated individually and quoted separately, and a custom domain costs about €10–15 a year direct from the registrar.

    Not sure which level?

    Tell me about your business and I will help you pick the right level. Thirty minutes, free, no pitch. You get a written recommendation afterwards.
